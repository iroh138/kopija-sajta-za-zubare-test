faqs:
  - q: How long do braces usually take?
    a: Most courses run 12 to 30 months. Your exact estimate is in your written plan after the consultation.
  - q: Do braces hurt?
    a: Teeth feel tender for a few days after fitting and after adjustments. Over-the-counter pain relief is usually enough.

Braces suit almost every type of crowding, spacing and bite problem, including complex cases that aligners cannot treat.

**What happens:** we scan your teeth, bond the brackets in one visit of about 90 minutes, and see you every 6–8 weeks for adjustments.

**Afterwards:** you wear a retainer to keep the result.
